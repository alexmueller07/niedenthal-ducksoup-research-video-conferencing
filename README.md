# Lab Video Call

A desktop app for running a three-person research video call: two participants talk to each other while a researcher watches from a hidden third seat. The researcher can subtly change how each participant's face and voice appear *to their partner* in real time — making them look a bit more or less happy, or sound a bit different — either by hand, with one-click presets, or through automatic rules. Every participant only ever sees their own real camera and their partner's modified view; the researcher sees everything, and the app records every video/audio stream plus a full log of what happened and when.
